## Hey, yo, hello! 🤓

I'm a PhD student at the University of Toronto (supervised by Dr. Stephanie Ameis), working in computational neuroscience with a focus on psychiatric conditions. I study how early-life adversity shapes the organization of brain networks, taking a developmental lens and using multivariate statistics and machine learning.

## Research Areas

- Early-life adversity, brain network organization and psychosis-spectrum symptoms in youth
- How displacement and disrupted education affect brain development in refugee children
- Predicting treatment outcomes in late-life depression and brain stimulation trials

## Research Methods

- Functional neuroimaging (fMRI and fNIRS) to study brain network organization and connectivity
- Integrating clinical, behavioural and neuroimaging data using multivariate and univariate statistics
- Building and validating predictive models on clinical datasets to better understand psychiatric disorders
